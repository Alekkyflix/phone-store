Privacy Policy

Tech Mobile Store KE — Last updated: June 2025

Introduction

Tech Mobile Store KE ("we", "our", "us") operates a smartphone retail service accessible via our website and WhatsApp messaging service. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information you provide when interacting with us.

By using our WhatsApp ordering service or visiting our website, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Information We Collect

We collect the following information when you interact with us:

  • WhatsApp profile name — to personalise responses
  • WhatsApp phone number — to process and confirm your order
  • Order details — phone model requested, price, and payment method
  • M-Pesa transaction reference — to confirm payment
  • Messages sent to our WhatsApp bot — processed to respond to your enquiry

We do not collect passwords, ID numbers, or financial account credentials.

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to product enquiries and provide pricing information
  • To process and confirm orders placed via WhatsApp
  • To initiate M-Pesa payment requests for purchases
  • To send order confirmations and receipts via WhatsApp
  • To maintain transaction records for our internal sales logs
  • To improve our service quality and response accuracy

WhatsApp Messaging

Our WhatsApp bot is powered by Meta's WhatsApp Cloud API. When you message us, your messages are processed through Meta's infrastructure in accordance with WhatsApp's Privacy Policy . We use an automated AI assistant to handle initial enquiries; human staff review orders before confirmation.

We will only send you WhatsApp messages related to your order or enquiry. We do not send unsolicited marketing messages.

M-Pesa Payments

Payment is processed via Safaricom's M-Pesa platform. We initiate an STK push to your registered M-Pesa phone number. We do not store your M-Pesa PIN or any sensitive payment credentials. Only the transaction reference number and amount are retained for record-keeping.

M-Pesa transactions are governed by Safaricom's Privacy Policy.

Data Storage & Retention

Order and transaction data is stored securely in a cloud database (Supabase / PostgreSQL) hosted on servers within the European Union. We retain transaction records for up to 2 years for accounting and dispute resolution purposes.

WhatsApp conversation history is not stored permanently on our systems beyond what is necessary to process your order.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties except as required to operate our service:

  • Meta / WhatsApp — to deliver messages (subject to their own policy)
  • Safaricom — to process M-Pesa payments
  • n8n — our automation platform that routes messages and triggers payments

We will disclose information to law enforcement only when legally required to do so.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Request deletion of your personal data from our records
  • Opt out of receiving WhatsApp messages from us at any time by replying STOP
  • Raise a complaint if you believe your data has been mishandled
